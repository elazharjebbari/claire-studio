A thematic layer over \corpus{} makes it possible to ask what a clause is about,
and not only whether it is unfair, and to measure how reliably it can be answered.
The cost of the format and
the effect of granularity are separable and of different orders, and the
consolidation that agreement alone would favour is ruled out by a legal constraint
no statistical criterion expresses.

\paragraph{Availability.}
We release the layer with its individual votes, arbitration trail and frozen gold
standard, the codebook and protocol, the frozen taxonomy projections with the
fingerprint under which every figure here was computed, and the annotation
platform used to produce it
\cite{stenetorp2012brat,klie2018inception,pei2022potato}. A running instance is at
\url{https://pactiva.legal}; code and data will be released at
\url{https://github.com/elazharjebbari/claire-studio} upon publication.
