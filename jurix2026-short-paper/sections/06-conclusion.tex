A thematic layer over \corpus{} makes it possible to ask what a clause is about,
and not only whether it is unfair. Built as a multi-label annotation by three
independent trained annotators, it also makes the reliability of that question
measurable: the cost of the multi-label format and the effect of taxonomy
granularity are separable, of different orders, and the consolidation that
agreement alone would favour is ruled out by a legal constraint that no statistical
criterion expresses.

\paragraph{Availability.}
We release the thematic layer with its individual annotator votes, the arbitration
trail and the frozen gold standard; the annotation codebook and protocol; the
frozen taxonomy projections (\Tn{20}, \Tn{14}, \Tn{11}, \Tn{10}) with the
fingerprint used for every figure in this paper; and the open-source annotation
platform on which the layer was produced, which supports browsing, measuring,
arbitrating, exporting and extending it under the same protocol
\cite{stenetorp2012brat,klie2018inception,pei2022potato}. \todo{DOI, licence and
