\section{Conclusion and Availability}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We have executed the grey list of Directive 93/13/EEC, a list of configurations of rights and obligations, as frozen queries over clause templates extracted automatically from the \corpus{} corpus. The result is a detector whose every decision cites the item it implements, and an item-by-item measurement of how much of the annotated unfairness a literal reading of the Annex retrieves: on held-out contracts, a precise but narrow fraction, about a third, below the clause theme alone and well below a learned encoder. The error analysis shows where literal execution fails and where the benchmark and the Annex part ways. With the companion short paper, which releases the thematic layer and its codebook, we release the queries with their hashes, the extracted templates, the code and the annotation platform.
