\section{Results B: The Price of Interpretability}
\label{sec:cost}
Table~\ref{tab:cost} compares the union of the frozen queries with three text-only systems on the same held-out sentences. The queries reach a binary $F_1$ of 0.36 [0.32; 0.42], with a precision of 0.41 (Wilson interval [0.36; 0.46]). The gap is $-0.34$ [$-0.40$; $-0.28$] against the fine-tuned legal encoder, $-0.17$ [$-0.21$; $-0.12$] against the lexical model and $-0.07$ [$-0.12$; $-0.02$] against the theme alone. Non-inferiority at the pre-registered margin of 0.05 is rejected in all three comparisons, and again on the second extraction pass, where the union reaches an $F_1$ of 0.34 [0.28; 0.41]. Of the 440 unfair sentences, the queries retrieve 145, of which the encoder also finds 123; 22 are found by the queries alone and 79 by neither system (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}).
